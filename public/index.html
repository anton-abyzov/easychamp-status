What does Operational mean?

Operational means the configured, current checks passed. It does not prove every feature works. Open a service to see exactly what is checked and what is outside its coverage.

Unknown means evidence is missing, expired, invalid, or a monitor cannot complete its check. Group coverage shows services with complete current checks, including checks that found an issue.

Daily bars summarize the worst observed status in each UTC day. Missing component evidence remains Unknown. These are sampled observations, not continuous uptime or an availability SLA.

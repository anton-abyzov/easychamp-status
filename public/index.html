What these checks mean

Each component shows the coverage actually checked. A page check validates content and bounded rendering; it does not prove every signed-in feature works. Import health requires a successful scheduled run and verified persisted destination data. A responding health endpoint alone cannot turn a feed green.

Unknown means evidence is missing, expired, invalid, or an automation policy prevents a check. Daily bars summarize observed checks, with unknown observations excluded from the success fraction. They are not a continuous availability SLA. Monitoring schedules can be delayed; evidence ages update even when collection stops.
